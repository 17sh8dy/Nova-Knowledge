# The Nova Account

A Nova Account is **optional**. The Nova products are built so that what already worked without an account keeps working
without one. An account adds things on top; it is not a gate in front of anything.

## Where an account can be used

Nova's websites (the Nova home page, Nova.Help, Nova Legal) have a sign-in. Nova.Review lets anyone read reviews and suggestions
without an account, and an account adds things such as editing your own reviews. The desktop apps that support it, such as Atlas
and Replay.GG, have a Nova Account section in their settings. In the apps, signing in is done through the browser: the app shows
a short code and you approve it on the Nova website, so the app never asks for your password.

## What it does not do

- Using Atlas, Replay.GG or Nova Cut does not require a Nova Account.
- Recordings and clips made with Replay.GG are not uploaded because of an account.

## Where to learn more

How personal data is handled is described in the Nova Privacy Policy and in the product-specific documents on Nova Legal. For
help with an account, use Nova.Help.
