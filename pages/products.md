# Nova products

## Available now

| Product | What it is | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Atlas** | A desktop assistant for Windows that works with your apps, windows and files, with local capabilities at the centre | https://atlas-website.shadylabs.workers.dev/ |
| **Nova Cut** | A desktop video and photo editor for Windows | https://nova-cut-site.shadylabs.workers.dev/ |
| **Replay.GG** | Gameplay recording, an instant-replay buffer and clipping, as a Windows installer | https://replay-gg-site.shadylabs.workers.dev/ |
| **Nova.Help** | Support: guides, and tickets answered by a person | https://nova-help.shadylabs.workers.dev/ |
| **Nova.Review** | Public reviews and suggestions for Nova products | https://nova-review.shadylabs.workers.dev/ |
| **Nova Legal** | Terms, privacy and product-specific legal documents | https://nova-legal.shadylabs.workers.dev/ |

## Not publicly available

Nova's home page lists **NovaOS** as coming soon and **Nova Games** as coming soon, and says these products are not publicly
available yet. This page makes no statement about when, or whether, they will be.

## Notes

- The Nova home page also lists **Nova.Donate** under Community. This page has no verified information about it.
- For a product's current version and what changed, use its own site or its release notes: they are more current than this page.
