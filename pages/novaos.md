# NovaOS

NovaOS is described on the Nova home page as "your desktop, reimagined": a fresh, connected workspace designed to make everyday
computing feel extraordinary. Nova lists it as **coming soon** and says it is not publicly available yet.

## What is not known here

There is no verified information on this page about NovaOS's release, features, platform support, price or licence. Please do not
treat NovaOS as available.
