# Atlas

Atlas is Nova's desktop assistant. Its website describes it as a desktop assistant "built to understand what you need, interact
with your computer, and adapt to the way you work". It is made to do things, not only answer, and it is built around local
capabilities. It is available as a Windows app.

## What it is for

- **Desktop control:** designed to work with the apps, windows, files and tools you actually use.
- **Automation:** turn a plain-language goal into a sequence of actions and repeatable workflows.
- **Customization:** shape the assistant around its name, voice, appearance, behaviour and how it runs actions.
- **A capability system:** built to gain new skills, integrations and workflows over time.

## Where it runs and what it sends

Atlas runs on your computer. The Atlas Terms on Nova Legal describe what Atlas does on your computer and what it sends to AI and
search services. Read them for the authoritative answer on privacy.
