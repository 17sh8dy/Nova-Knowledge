# Nova Legal

Nova Legal is one place to find the status and legal information for Nova products: terms, privacy and product-specific documents.

## What it says about itself

- Nothing in Nova Legal has been approved or reviewed by an attorney. That does not remove the need to follow, agree to and accept
  the terms, rules or notices presented when using a Nova product.
- A document marked "Content pending" has not been written yet and is not in force.
- Documents marked "In force" carry a version and an effective date.
- English is the original of every document; other languages are translations.

## What is there

Terms of Service, Terms of Usage, and terms specific to individual products, including Atlas, Replay.GG and Nova Cut. For what a
document currently says, read it on Nova Legal: it is the authority, not this page.
