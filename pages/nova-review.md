# Nova.Review

Nova.Review is where people read and write reviews, and make suggestions, for Nova products. Nova.Help lists it as a website
covering reviews and suggestions for Nova products.

## Accounts

Signing in with a Nova Account is optional. Without an account you can read reviews. An account adds things such as managing your
own reviews. See the accounts page.
