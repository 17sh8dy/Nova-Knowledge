## Getting help

Use Nova.Help: choose the product, answer a few questions, and a ticket is filed with what is needed attached. You get a ticket ID
to reopen it later. A person reads each ticket. For business inquiries, or something you could not resolve there, Nova.Help gives a
contact address on its own page.

## Reviews and suggestions

Nova.Review lets people read reviews and suggestions for Nova products.
