# Nova.Help

Nova.Help is Nova's support site. You choose the product you are having trouble with, answer a few questions, and a ticket is
filed with what Nova needs already attached.

## Products it covers

Nova (the website and studio), Nova Legal, Nova.Review, Nova Cut, Atlas (the desktop assistant), the Atlas website, and Replay.GG.
Each has its own areas and topics.

## How a ticket works

- You get a ticket ID immediately. Keep it: it is how you reopen the ticket, together with the email address you used.
- A person reads the ticket and either answers it or asks for what is missing.
- The ticket stays open until you say it is done, or it is resolved and closed.
- You can read replies, add information and see where it stands.

## Other contact

Nova.Help lists a contact address on its own page for business inquiries or issues you could not resolve there.
