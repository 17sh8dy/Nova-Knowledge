# Nova Cut

Nova Cut is a desktop video and photo editor for creators who want professional tools without putting essential editing features
behind a subscription. It is available for Windows (x64), and its site lists it as free under the MIT licence.

## What it includes

Two editors in one application:

- **Video editing:** a multi-track, non-destructive timeline with split, trim, ripple delete, snapping, effects and transitions.
  Its site lists 53 effects across seven categories and 18 transitions, which run as GPU shaders.
- **Photo editing:** a layer-based image editor with layers, groups and masks, for thumbnails, banners and social posts.

## What Nova says about pricing and AI

Nova says editing features stay free: no trial period, no watermark, and no editing feature that stops working when you stop paying.

Nova Cut has no AI features today. Its site says that AI features, if they arrive, may be offered as optional paid services
alongside the editor, never as a fence around it. That is a statement of intent, not a release.
