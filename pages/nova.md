# What Nova is

Nova is an independent software studio. Its own description is "Create what's next. Thoughtful tools for big ideas."
It makes a family of software; each product is built to stand on its own and to be better because the others exist.

## What Nova makes

Nova groups its products into categories:

- AI and computing: **Atlas** and **NovaOS**
- Creative tools: **Nova Cut** and **Replay.GG**
- Gaming: **Nova Games**
- Community: **Nova.Donate** and **Nova.Review**
- Help and support: **Nova.Help**

Which of these are available today is listed on the products page.

## The principles Nova states

Nova describes four positions on its home page: *Software should be owned, not rented.*

1. **Local first.** Your work happens on your hardware, in formats you can open anywhere.
2. **Calm interfaces.** Tools appear when the moment calls for them, then step back.
3. **Own the foundations.** Where the platform sets the ceiling, Nova builds it itself.
4. **No shortcuts.** Architecture chosen for the codebase that exists in five years.
